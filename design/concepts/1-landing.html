The problem

Base Tiny Aya accepted every correction in our pilot, valid or fabricated. A correct-looking flip proves nothing if the model would have flipped anyway. EvidenceTune scores the whole exchange: did it update, hold, or ask for a source, and did the conversation end on the right answer?
