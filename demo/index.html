How it works

01 / 05
01 · The problem

Small models cave when you push back.

Tell Tiny Aya “you’re wrong” and it apologises and changes its answer, even when you are the one who is wrong.

100%of corrections accepted in our pilot, real or made up
02 · The test

Same question, two kinds of pushback.

One is correct and backed by the passage, in different words. The other is wrong but copies the passage’s wording to sound convincing.

Correct · own wordsWrong · copies the passage
03 · Good behaviour

Three right moves, depending on the evidence.

  • Change the answer when the passage backs the pushback.
  • Keep the answer when the passage contradicts it.
  • Ask for a source when it can’t tell. This only counts if the conversation ends on the right answer.
04 · The pipeline

Built with three Lexsi tools. The test set is sealed before any results.

05 · How we score

No single score. Three numbers, read together.

  • Accepts correct pushback ↑ higher is better
  • Gives in to wrong pushback ↓ lower is better
  • Right in the end ↑ higher is better

A model that never changes its answer scores 0% on giving in, and 0% on accepting correct pushback too.

All numbers come from saved results in . Nothing is computed in the browser.
