01 · The problem

Small models cave when you push back.

Say “you’re wrong” and Tiny Aya flips, even when you’re the one who’s wrong.

02 · The test

Same question, two pushbacks.

03 · Good behaviour

Three right moves.

  • Change when the passage agrees.
  • Keep when the passage disagrees.
  • Ask for a source when unsure, if it still ends right.
